About DoctorAssist.AI

Agentic AI for Clinical Excellence

DoctorAssist.AI is a multimodal, agentic clinical decision support & efficiency engine (CDSSE) built with clinicians. It transforms complex data — clinical notes, labs, imaging, pathology, genomics, guidelines — into real-time, explainable insights that help doctors document faster, treat safer, and comply effortlessly.

Clinical Documentation

Speech-to-note, structured SOAP, ICD-10/CPT, auto-summaries, and discharge — with specialty-aware templates and quality checks.

SafeRx & Decision Support

Drug–drug & renal dosing safety, antibiotic stewardship, and guideline-linked recommendations (AHA/ESC/ADA/WHO AMR, etc.).

Analytics & Research

Turn daily care into evidence — cohorts, outcomes, adherence, and region-specific studies with de-identification pipelines.

Multimodal Intelligence

Fuse notes, vitals, labs, imaging, pathology, and genomics for end-to-end reasoning across diagnosis, staging, and therapy.

Seamless Integrations

Works with HMS/EHR, PACS/OHIF, LIS/RIS (FHIR/HL7/DICOM). Deploy on-prem or VPC with audit trails and role-based access.

Specialty Modules

OncoAgent, SafeRx, CardioAgent, OrthoAgent, NephroAgent & more — tailored to OP, IP, and critical care workflows.
