# Introduction to Pulmonology & Core Medical Concepts

## 1. What is Pulmonology?
**Pulmonology** (also known as Respiratory Medicine or Respirology) is a medical specialty dealing with diseases of the **respiratory tract**, including the lungs, airways (trachea, bronchi, bronchioles), pulmonary vascular bed, chest wall, and respiratory muscles.

---

## 2. Core Physiological Concepts

### A. Ventilation vs. Gas Exchange
- **Ventilation**: The mechanical movement of air into and out of the lungs (inspiration and expiration).
- **Gas Exchange (Respiration)**: The transfer of **Oxygen ($\text{O}_2$)** from alveolar air into pulmonary capillary blood, and **Carbon Dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$)** from capillary blood into the alveoli.

### B. Obstructive vs. Restrictive Lung Diseases
Pulmonary medicine categorizes chronic lung diseases primarily into two major physiological patterns based on Pulmonary Function Testing (PFT):

| Feature | Obstructive Lung Disease | Restrictive Lung Disease |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Problem** | Difficulty exhaling air completely due to narrowed airways. | Inability to fully expand lungs with air (reduced lung volume). |
| **Key Examples** | Asthma, COPD (Emphysema & Chronic Bronchitis), Bronchiectasis. | Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD), Pulmonary Fibrosis, Kyphoscoliosis. |
| **Spirometry Pattern** | **Reduced $\text{FEV}_1/\text{FVC}$ Ratio** ($<70\%$ or $< \text{LLN}$). | Normal/Increased Ratio, but **Reduced Total Lung Capacity (TLC)** and **Reduced FVC**. |

---

## 3. Major Clinical Sub-Specialties & Disease Categories

### 1. Obstructive Airway Diseases (Asthma & COPD)
- **Asthma**: Chronic inflammatory disorder characterized by variable, reversible airflow limitation, airway hyper-responsiveness, and bronchospasm.
- **COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease)**: Progressive, largely irreversible airflow limitation caused by long-term exposure to noxious particles (primarily tobacco smoke).
  - *Chronic Bronchitis*: Chronic cough and sputum production for $\ge 3$ months in 2 consecutive years.
  - *Emphysema*: Destruction of gas-exchanging alveolar walls leading to air trapping.

### 2. Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD) & Pulmonary Fibrosis
- A heterogeneous group of $>200$ disorders causing inflammation and progressive scarring (fibrosis) of the lung parenchyma (interstitium).
- Lead to stiff lungs, reduced oxygen diffusion ($\text{DLCO}$), and exertional hypoxia.
- Key condition: **IPF (Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis)**.

### 3. Sleep-Disordered Breathing & Sleep Medicine
- **Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA)**: Repetitive upper airway collapse during sleep resulting in pauses in breathing (apneic events), oxygen desaturations, and sleep fragmentation.
- **Central Sleep Apnea (CSA)**: Lack of respiratory effort driven by the brain's central nervous system control.

## 4. Key Medical Terminology Glossary

| Medical Term | Pronunciation / Abbr. | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dyspnea** | *disp-NEE-uh* | Shortness of breath or difficulty breathing. |
| **Hypoxia** | *high-POX-ee-uh* | Deficiency of oxygen reaching body tissues. |
| **Hypoxemia** | *high-pock-SEE-mee-uh* | Low partial pressure of oxygen in arterial blood ($\text{SpO}_2 < 90\%$). |
| **Hypercapnia** | *high-per-KAP-nee-uh* | Abnormally elevated carbon dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$) levels in the blood. |
| **Hemoptysis** | *hee-MOP-tih-sis* | Coughing up blood or blood-tinged sputum from the respiratory tract. |
| **Spirometry** | *spy-ROM-uh-tree* | Standard diagnostic test measuring airflow volumes and rates. |
| **HRCT** | High-Res CT | High-Resolution Computed Tomography of the chest to visualize lung parenchyma. |
| **Bronchoscopy** | *bron-KOS-ko-pee* | Endoscopic procedure visualizing the tracheobronchial tree using a flexible scope. |
| **Thoracentesis** | *thor-uh-sen-TEE-sis* | Procedure to insert a needle into the pleural space to drain fluid (pleural effusion). |
